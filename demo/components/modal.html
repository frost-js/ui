Alpine Circuit briefing

Before departure

Check the park alert board on the morning of travel. Snow or high wind can close the upper ridge with little notice.

Route conditions

The first six kilometres follow a formed trail. Beyond the saddle, the route crosses exposed rock and requires confident navigation in low cloud.

Water and equipment

Carry all drinking water. Each walker should bring warm layers, rain protection, a head torch, and an offline map of the complete circuit.

Turnaround plan

If the group has not reached North Saddle by 1:30 pm, return by the ascent route. Do not continue across the ridge after that time.

Emergency contacts

Leave the route plan and expected return time with a trusted contact. Mobile coverage is unreliable beyond the trailhead.
