Popovers

Title and content

Popovers display an optional title and a content body when their trigger is activated.

data-ui-title data-ui-content

HTML content

HTML can add simple structure or emphasis and is sanitized by default.

data-ui-html="true"

Placement

Request a preferred side and keep it fixed when the surrounding layout guarantees enough space.

data-ui-placement="*"

Alignment

Position selects start, center, or end alignment without changing the chosen side.

data-ui-position="start|center|end"

Dismissal and disabled controls

Focus-triggered popovers dismiss on blur; disabled controls require an interactive wrapper.

data-ui-trigger="focus"
