Toasts

Anatomy

Combine an optional header with a concise message and a dismissal control.

.toast .toast-header .toast-body
F Route planner Just now
Your coastal itinerary is ready to review.

Live notification

Initialize a hidden toast and call its show method in response to an application event.

UI.Toast.init(node).show()
✓ Route saved Just now
Southern edge traverse was added to your saved routes.

Frosted surface

The toast uses a translucent background and backdrop blur so underlying content remains partially visible.

.toast
F Field notes 2 min ago
Offline notes are available for this section of the route.

Stacking

Keep related notifications aligned and evenly separated in a toast container.

.toast-container
✓ Offline maps ready Now
Three map regions were downloaded to this device.
! Weather update 4 min ago
Rain is now expected on the exposed ridge after 3 pm.

Body content and actions

Use a body on its own for short messages, or add compact actions when a response is useful.

.toast-body
Your equipment checklist is up to date.
A newer route briefing is available.

Semantic variants

Text, background, and border utilities can give a toast a semantic status color.

.text-bg-{variant} .border-0
A guide has joined your trip workspace.
