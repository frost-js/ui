Floating Labels

Filled floating labels

Place the label after the control inside a form-input wrapper.

.input-filled .input-floating + .label-floating

Sizes and groups

Floating labels respond to input and input-group sizing.

.input-sm .input-lg .input-group
Required

Outline floating labels

The label occupies the outlined boundary when the control contains a value.

.input-outline .input-floating + .label-floating

Sizes and groups

The same modifiers preserve label alignment in outlined controls.

.input-sm .input-lg .input-group
Required
