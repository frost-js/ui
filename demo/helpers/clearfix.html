Clearfix

Containing floated content

Add clearfix to a parent when floated content should continue to define the parent’s height.

.clearfix .float-start

Coastal headland

The image is floated for a compact editorial treatment. Clearfix keeps the card body wrapped around both the image and this longer description.

Opposing actions

Floats can also separate simple controls while the clearfix preserves the surrounding action bar.
