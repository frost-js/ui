Color & Background

Semantic pairings

Each helper applies a background together with its intended contrast color, so the pairing remains readable across themes.

.text-bg-{color}
Primary.text-bg-primary
Accent.text-bg-accent
Secondary.text-bg-secondary
Success.text-bg-success
Info.text-bg-info
Warning.text-bg-warning
Danger.text-bg-danger
Light.text-bg-light
Dark.text-bg-dark
