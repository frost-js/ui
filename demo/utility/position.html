Edge offsets

Top, end, bottom, and start utilities accept 0%, 50%, and 100%. Logical start and end adapt to writing direction.

.top-{0|50|100} .end-{0|50|100} .bottom-{0|50|100} .start-{0|50|100}
top-0 start-0 top-0 end-0 top-50 start-50 bottom-50 end-50 bottom-0 start-0 bottom-0 end-0
