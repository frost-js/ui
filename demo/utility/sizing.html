Maximum and minimum constraints

Cap media at its parent size, or remove the automatic minimum that can prevent flex and grid children from shrinking.

.max-w-100 .max-h-100 .min-w-0 .min-h-0
.max-w-100
This element requests more height than its parent but is capped at 100%.
.max-h-100
A flex child with min-width zero can shrink and truncate this deliberately long route title.
